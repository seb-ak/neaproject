click to start game

controls
  • WASD to move
  • Space to jump
  • E to interact
  • Escape to pause game
hints
  • hold jump to go higher
  • tap jump to go less high
  • hold up and tap jump in air to double jump
  • hold left or right and tap jump in air to dash
  • hold down and tap jump in air to groud slam
  • hold S to crouch
  • crouch whilst running to slide
  • jump whilst sliding to slide jump
